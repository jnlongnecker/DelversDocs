# Simple Roguelike

- Depth from simplicity
- Very few "buttons" to use:
    - Move (doubles as attack)
    - Quicken (hold while you move to modify move)
    - Alt function/offhand (hold to use this when you move)
- The depth comes from interactions of systems
    - Weapons have a "swing"
    - Temporary buffs greatly help in diving a dungeon
    - Status effects have a buildup and are extremely debilitating
    - Creatures have "sides" that attacking them from is more or less effective
    - Creatures have patterns that they use in combat
- Ideally, the player doesn't take damage
- They can use many things to their advantage in order to take as little damage as possible
    - Stuns from crits
        - Crits can activate in specific conditions unique to the weapon
    - Openings in an enemy's pattern
    - Openings from parries/blocks
