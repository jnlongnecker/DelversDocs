# Axioms

- There exists the state of nothing
- Nothing-ness is infinite
- Interaction works on restrictions & trade-offs; equivalent exchange
- Random, infinite rules are instated

# The Universe

A universe is defined as a configuration of equal exchange rules set in place. There exist infinite universes, each one a configuration of rules. As there are infinite universes, there are also infinite instances of each universe, and each universe is perfectly deterministic and will end up the same as all the others unless modified by a higher dimension.

Our particular universe has the following rules:

- When energy becomes zero, all infinities aggregate into a higher dimension
- The rules governing physics

In the state of nothing, defined as the null dimension, there is no energy and no matter. The first rule then is enacted, resulting in infinite points defined as the 0th dimension. The 0th dimension has no energy, so again is infinitely aggregated into the 1st dimension. As the points along the 1st dimension exist over time, some will collect into points of infinite mass (a black hole). Each instance of a black hole becomes an individual instance of infinity in that dimension, which will then be aggregated when energy becomes zero. This in effect turns each black hole into an individual particle in a higher dimension. This continues infinitely, across every dimension.

Imagine each particle as a point on a graph. Imagine the origin of each dimension as the origin point of a graph. If you graph the position of each particle as its distance from the origin at a given point in time, you get a comprehensive graph of the life of each dimension. Time begins at the origin (Big Bang), and time ends at the heat death of the dimension. At the heat death, the first rule activates and the higher dimension has its own "Big Bang", with each black hole a particle in the higher dimension.

Life begins and ends because it is an emergent property of our current universe. Due to the initial cyclical rule, dimensions are created and these dimensions exhibit similar cyclical properties; life being one amongst them.

# Magic

Magic is the intelligent utilization of the first rule by individual actors. Beings of a higher dimension can reach into a lower dimension and manually aggregate a new infinity. By doing this, an additional infinity will exist post-heat death, which is meant to correspond to an individual particle in the higher dimension. Normally, this never happens as at the heat death, no more interaction can occur. Higher dimensional beings are able to break this convention.

What occurs is that a new particle comes into being for each newly aggregated infinity. The position of the particle depends on the following details:

- Which dimension the new infinity occurred in
- The location of the infinity in that dimension

Higher dimensional beings can utilize this to create particles and configure them in a specific way to produce phenomena in their dimension. For example, a lump of iron is a group of subatomic particles arranged into iron and other alloy atoms in a particular configuration. If this configuration is replicated by producing specific infinities at specific places in specific dimensions, a lump of iron will spontaneously come into existence at a given position.

Obviously, this is so incredibly difficult it borderlines impossible. While higher dimensional beings often accidentally create lower dimensional infinities as they go about their day, with no intelligence to this creation it simply results in particles existing for a fraction of a nanosecond before snuffing themselves from existence. Therefore, people do not actually notice the "magic" they or anything else will do on a day by day basis.

Beings of a higher dimension are able to produce lower dimensions with particular rules.

Fundamentally, a number is a 0th dimensional entity. It can also be referred to as "information". Mathematically, 0 is not a number because it is not information, it is the mathematical representation of nothing. Thus, anything natural must a positive number (as negative would imply a reverse of the information), and is between 0 and infinity. Infinity also is not a number, and thus not information. It is the opposite of nothing, and all information exists between nothing and infinity; the two binaries of non-information.

In the natural number line between 0 and infinity, there exists the first infinity. Since you can shift this in any direction and it is just as large, consider 0 to be the middle of this infinity, which represents the first dimension. Add another of these infinities and you get the second dimension. Keep adding infinities and you get higher and higher dimensions with each infinity you add.

Inside each of these individual infinities, there exist smaller infinities within them. Places where a new infinity of information converges. Consider an infinite conversion within two points of information as a "black hole". Each black hole represents an individual particle in a higher dimension. If that particle converges with an infinity in another dimension, it becomes a particle in the next higher dimension.

As there becomes infinitely many numbers on the line, they will begin to converge at certain points towards another infinity. This creation of another infinity results in a new dimension. If you follow the newly created infinity, it will itself have another infinity within it, which creates a yet higher dimension. It is therefore an emergent property of information to:

- Converge towards infinity

Information which represents our world, the 3rd dimension, utilizes real numbers. However, as the real numbers tend towards an infinity, they lose their ability to accurately estimate the value of the information there. We cannot see the effect of these infinities with our eyes, because our eyes can only detect 3 dimensional phenomena. However, we can calculate the result that this higher dimensional interaction takes on our dimension.

In other words, physical infinities that are possible to reach are where we interact with a higher dimension. The only known physical infinity that can be reached are black holes. To us, being able to see the information of the 3rd dimension, it is obvious where 2nd and 1st dimensional infinities will converge. However, we lack the perspective to be able to see where 3rd dimension infinities will converge: again at a midway point. Black holes represent some midway point between where information converges to a new infinity and a place where information will never converge; the anchor point to be in between. This is matter (information) that, at the heat death of the universe, does not end up in the new infinity.

We cannot, however, see where the 1st dimensional midpoint is, since we can only "see" our dimension, the dimension higher than us and the dimension lower than us.

When this information converges with another dimension of information, we get our current understood universe. The big bang is the origin point of this conversion, each particle representing information that is attempting to converge with other information. As that information converges over time, what emerges is our 3rd dimensional universe. The end of all time (the 4th dimension) awaits at either the black hole (convergence) or the heat death (divergence). Ideally, our time ends before that point.

The goal of information is to be the an anchor between itself and another infinity and create as much additional information as possible. By creating new information, you create new infinities which then are more likely to converge somewhere with another dimension. If information wasn't living, it doesn't create much information and is less likely to converge with another dimension (reach the black hole).

For example, a rock doesn't do much. It only works with a few different kinds of information. You can't ask it what its favorite color is. And yet, it might get lucky. That configuration of particles might just happen to reach a singularity. However, living things create *new* information, meaning life is an anchor of a new infinity.

Take, again, 0 and 1. 1 lives on in a higher dimension as information, because it serves as the anchor for the new information that is 0.5 as 0.5 is the midpoint between 0 and 1. In the pursuit of creating new information, particles need to produce more and more new phenomena. A living being is not only described by many different particles over time as pieces of the whole break down, die, and are recycled, but also invents new and never before seen information like words, emotions, and favorite things.

Said a different way, if you apply a second dimension (width) onto the first (length) by taking these infinite subdivisions, you get 2 dimensional information. Again apply infinite "2 dimensional planes" on top of each other (height) and you get 3 dimensions. Apply infinite 3 dimensional planes on each other, and you get the 4th dimension of time.

Life is simply an emergent property of 4 dimensions: length, width, height, and time. If you have these properties, life emerges over time just like interesting mathematical phenomena emerge in numbers; and for the same reason: To create more information. Just as certain numbers belong in certain mathematical sets, so too do living things begin and end; include some information and not others. Our universe can now be understood as simply all of the information that can exist in 4 dimensions.

If we understand as all the natural numbers as all the information can that exist in 1 dimension, all real numbers as all the information that can exist in 2 dimensions, we can then understand our universe as all the information that can exist in 3 dimensions. As the information grows larger and larger towards infinity, we find more and more of it. This is due to information creating new information by simply existing. 

In order to continue to create new information towards infinite information, information must tend towards "newness" or else it will stagnate. We know that information does have this property because an infinity exists. Because information has this property, it will evolve. Evolution is not just a property of what we call "life", it is a property of information itself. Evolution is more foundational than life, evolution can even describe the particles making up living and non-living things. Evolution can describe even the information that makes up the particles. You can think of the start of evolution as the origin of a new dimension.

Since life is the origin point of a new "evolution", we know that in order to reach the 5th dimension, particles must at some point be a part of a living being. Since being alive is the condition that information must attain in order to become a part of the 5th dimension, life is the property that gets added to the prior 4 dimensions of length, width, height and time; we can also refer to life as the average of all 4th dimensional information. From the evolution of life emerges consciousness. We can now consider consciousness as a 6th dimensional phenomena. 

We are able to observe the effects of a dimension lower and a dimension higher than the dimensions we exist in, but can only observe the dimensions we exist in. We exist in multiple dimensions: the 3rd, the 4th, and the 5th. We are able to observe into the 2nd dimension and easily see where convergencies happen. We are able to experience the 3rd dimension of matter (where the 1st, 2nd and 3rd dimensions converge) and where it moves over time. Where time will be infinite, life emerges. Since we are living, we are complete with the 5th dimension. Since we end at the 5th dimension, consciousness is the point where life becomes infinite.

Our being has already shed its 2nd dimensionality. It constantly sheds its 3rd dimensionality over time; a property of life. In order to fully realize our 4th dimensionality, it needs to shed its "time", or somehow exist forever. As our body constantly recycles itself over and over, living and dying constantly, the consciousness changes over cycles. Progress is no longer measured over time, it's measured in the progression of consciousness.

When a living thing finally does something that creates information that will itself infinitely create new, fractal information, that consciousness making up the person will finally be able to shed its 4th dimensionality as it's no longer necessary; the consciousness has reached its peak and has created reached infinite consciousness. If they cannot do this, their consciousness is "reborn" in a new life, which will then attempt to once more create information that will exist forever. It is the purpose of life to end this cycle to give rise to their higher dimensional self. Consciousness is the property that emerges when life becomes infinite; life is the property that emerges when time becomes infinite, time is the property that emerges when height becomes infinite, height is the property that emerges when width becomes infinite, width is the property that emerges when length becomes infinite, length is the property that emerges when information becomes infinite, and information is the property that emerges when nothing becomes infinite.
