# Time Wizard

The player plays as a time wizard; a powerful mage who specializes in time manipulation. They utilize astral projection to discover new places to look for research materials in relative safety. Death of the projection simply brings themselves back to their main body.

The player enters a rift in order to collect research, destroy monsters, and gain treasures from different times. Their end goal is to research a way to rewind time in order to fix awful calamities that caused the world to get to the point where it is now.

The player has a wand that can take many forms:

- Axe
- Sword
- Lance
- Warhammer
- Shield

The wand can be upgraded during the run by spending mana, and can be upgraded before the run by attaching runes.

Other players can play as one of the time wizard's simulacrum and group up.

The gameplay loop is the following:

- Pick a zone to begin a run
- Gain resources in the zone and fight enemies
- Upgrade powers
- End run

While playing in the zone, non-player actors will link themselves to one of the players. When that player acts, the non-player actors will also act. Some non-player actors are linked to all players, such as traps and bosses.

Players use resources in order to perform research, which allows them to unlock new options, new runes, new zones, and new cosmetics & minigames.

Each run is divided up into levels. Between levels, you can spend condensed mana collected from each level to temporarily upgrade yourself. This is only possible due to the ethereal nature of the projection and these powers get severed when the projection ends.
